# Geographic data

Country outlines: Natural Earth, 1:110m Admin-0 Countries, public-domain data.
Project page: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Retrieved for this update. Only English names and geometry are retained in src/data/countries.json.
Natural Earth uses de facto boundaries. This is a general geographic visualization, not an aviation chart.
City positions are inherited from the supplied Skybreak source; the metadata has not been independently verified. The 3D flight world remains procedural and compressed.
