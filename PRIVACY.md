# Privacy & Safety Policy — Skybreak Flight Combat

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Skybreak is designed from the ground up to be completely private, safe, and secure for pilots of all ages.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
- **No Personal Information**: Skybreak does not require an account, email address, name, or phone number.
- **No Third-Party Trackers**: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, tracking beacons, or ad networks.
- **No Advertising**: Skybreak contains no ads, sponsored promotions, or monetization tracking.

---

## 2. 100% Client-Side Architecture
- All 3D rendering (WebGL), procedural map generation, sound synthesis (Web Audio API), and aerodynamic flight physics run purely inside your local web browser.
- No gameplay data or flight telemetry is ever transmitted over the network.

---

## 3. Local Storage Policy
- Pilot preferences (selected flight controls, graphics quality, audio volume levels, inverted controls) are stored strictly inside your browser's private `localStorage` on your own device.
- You can clear this data at any time by resetting browser site data or clearing your browser cache.

---

## 4. Hardware Permissions & Security Policy
Skybreak operates under a strict **Content Security Policy (CSP)** and **Permissions Policy**:
- ❌ **Camera**: Access blocked (`camera=()`)
- ❌ **Microphone**: Access blocked (`microphone=()`)
- ❌ **Geolocation**: Access blocked (`geolocation=()`)
- ❌ **Payment APIs**: Access blocked (`payment=()`)
- ❌ **USB / HID**: Access blocked (`usb=()`)

---

## 5. Age & Compliance Safety (COPPA & GDPR)
- **COPPA Compliant**: Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act safe. No children's data is gathered.
- **GDPR Compliant**: General Data Protection Regulation safe. Zero user profiling, zero persistent cross-site identifiers.

---

## 6. Contact & Open Source Integrity
Skybreak is committed to open, transparent, and respectful software development.
