A browser flight game

A little more sky.
A lot more freedom.

Skybreak is a 3D arcade flight game built with Three.js. Explore the map in Free Flight, fly combat missions, or connect to a multiplayer server.

Choose your flight

  • Free Flight with terrain, airbases, landing gear and approach aids.
  • Three combat missions with cannon, missiles and flares.
  • Five aircraft, liveries and modifications unlocked through local progression.
  • Keyboard, mouse, touch and standard gamepad input; Assisted and Manual flight modes.

The world and aircraft are simplified game representations. This is entertainment, not a flight-training or navigation tool. Geographic labels do not imply political or military affiliation.

Multiplayer requires an available, compatible server. A server connection is separate from the static game host.
