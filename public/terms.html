The game

Skybreak is an experimental browser flight and combat game for entertainment. Aircraft behavior, navigation aids, locations and combat are simplified. Do not use it for real aviation training or navigation.

Fair play

Do not harass other players, impersonate others, interfere with servers or exploit bugs to disrupt matches. Use a suitable display name and respect other players. Multiplayer availability and moderation depend on the server operator.

Saved progress & availability

Progress and settings are saved locally. They can be lost if you reset storage, clear browser data or change devices. There is no guaranteed cloud backup. Features and compatibility may change as the game is developed; uninterrupted availability is not promised.

Content & third-party software

Skybreak uses Three.js and other open-source dependencies under their respective licences. Aircraft and scene visuals in this build are procedural. No third-party GLB aircraft asset is bundled. Real-world names do not imply endorsement or affiliation.
