Takeoff & landing

Hold W to increase throttle. Throttle stays where you leave it. Steer on the runway with Q/D. Once above 55 m/s, press ↑ to raise the nose, then retract gear with G. Space fires the cannon.

For landing, lower gear, reduce throttle with S and align with the runway. B holds the brake. Keep the wings level, descent gentle and speed low. L opens airbase information and approach aids.

Mouse & gamepad

Select a device in Settings → Controls. Mouse steering has sensitivity, deadzone, response curve and optional pointer lock. Capture starts only after clicking the flight view. Hold MMB to look around without steering; Z recentres the stick.

Standard controller: left stick pitch/roll, right stick look, shoulders yaw, RT cannon, LT brake, A missile, B flares, X next target, Y camera. D-pad up/down changes throttle, left opens the map, right toggles gear; left-stick click boosts and Menu pauses. Button names vary by controller. Use the calibration preview for drift and support status.

Touch

Use the left stick, throttle slider and labelled action buttons. Multiple touches let you steer and fire together. Release, cancellation or loss of focus clears held input. In portrait, optional telemetry is collapsed to preserve the flight view.

Open Skies · Aegis Strait

Lead two persistent wingmen through three waves: 3 patrol fighters, 4 reinforcements, then VIPER. Y opens squadron orders and pauses the battle. Inside that panel only: 1 Cover me, 2 Attack my target, 3 Regroup. Esc or Y closes it. Custom bindings appear in-game. Touch WING or controller View/Back opens the same panel; D-pad up/down selects, A confirms and B closes.

Up Arrow = nose up; Down Arrow = nose down. Gentler Open Skies steering is enabled by default in Assisted mode. Arrows bank and turn; release to level. Settings → Controls can disable the gentler response; Manual keeps full control. R selects a hostile; keep it near the reticle until LOCKED, then E / RMB launches (one press per missile). X deploys flares. Friendly aircraft use cyan circles; hostile diamonds turn green when locked. H reopens the flight guide and guided hints.

Completion gives 50 medal points, accuracy up to 20, avoiding cumulative damage up to 20, and surviving wingmen up to 10. Silver starts at 65; Gold at 85. Completion is required; no shots means zero accuracy points. Mission XP and medal XP are awarded once per sortie. Best scores are stored locally per difficulty. Recorded replay stores aircraft state and events; replay files and highlight images can be exported. Restart and Fly again preserve the chosen seed, difficulty and mission/campaign context. Clear midday lighting is temporary for this coastal sortie.

Troubleshooting

Skybreak needs WebGL 2 and browser graphics acceleration. If startup fails, enable graphics acceleration or try another supported browser. Choose Low quality on slower devices. Multiplayer needs a secure configured server on hosted HTTPS pages.

Tab away to pause a solo sortie. Online matches continue while menus are open or a player is disconnected. If motion feels uncomfortable, enable Reduced motion in Settings → HUD, or set Camera shake and Speed FOV to zero. HUD scale and high contrast are in Settings. Public help and policy pages work without starting the 3D game.
